Welcome. If you found your way here, you probably came from somewhere louder. That's fine. You can leave the noise at the door.

This site is meant to feel less like a feed and more like a room: a desk by a window, a lamp on, the fog just starting to lift off the trees outside. There are no pop-ups here, nothing blinking, nothing asking for your attention. Just writing, when there's something worth writing.

## What you'll find

Mostly longer notes — things I'm thinking through, working on, or slowly learning. Some will be finished. Some won't. The [What I'm Working On](/now/) page is where I keep a short, honest account of what's on the desk right now.

> The best rooms are the ones you don't want to leave.

## How to use it

There isn't much to it. Read something. Close the tab. Come back when you feel like it. If you'd like to follow along without checking in, there's an [RSS feed](/rss.xml) — the quietest way to subscribe to anything.
